## In short

- Meta says Hyperion, in Richland Parish, Louisiana, will reach five gigawatts of computing capacity and cost more than $50 billion. A Meta spokesperson told Quartz the project should reach two gigawatts by 2030, with no timeline for the full five. Bloomberg reported the $50 billion excludes chips and that the total with chips would be about $250 billion.
- Entergy Louisiana plans ten gas plants for the campus: three approved in 2025 (about 2.3 GW) and seven announced in March 2026 (more than 5,200 MW), plus about 240 miles of 500-kV lines and batteries at three sites.
- The first of the new plants, Waterford 5, is now projected at $1.755 billion, up 63 percent from $1.079 billion, according to WWNO. A preliminary estimate; a consumer advocate said such estimates can be off by 10 to 40 percent.

## What we could not verify

The calculations behind Meta's job and power figures are not public: the commission voted 3 to 1 on August 12 to overrule a judge's order to explain them, and Meta argues they are trade secrets. The headline numbers come from Meta, Entergy and state officials. We asked Meta to review the figures; no reply by publication.
