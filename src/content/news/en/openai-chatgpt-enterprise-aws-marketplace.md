## What happened

On 9 November 2026 OpenAI announced that ChatGPT Enterprise is available in AWS Marketplace. We read the announcement on 10 November 2026. The details below are OpenAI's, as stated on its page.

## Key details

- **How it works:** organizations can discover, buy and manage ChatGPT Enterprise through their existing AWS procurement and billing relationship, and can apply eligible spend to their AWS commitments.
- **Same product:** OpenAI says AWS customers get the same models and features as customers who buy directly, with new features arriving at the same time.
- **Scale figure:** OpenAI says more than 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT every week. This is OpenAI's own number.
- **Related offerings:** the listing complements OpenAI models already available in Amazon Bedrock. Eligible customers can also reach ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Financial Services through AWS Marketplace.
- **Quote:** the post includes a statement from Matt Yanchyshyn, AWS vice president for AWS Marketplace and partner services. Customers are told to contact their OpenAI or AWS account team about eligibility.
- **Not stated:** prices, supported regions, the date from which spend counts toward commitments, and how the listing relates to OpenAI's relationship with Microsoft and Azure.

## Why it matters

For companies that already commit spend to AWS, buying through the marketplace can put ChatGPT Enterprise on the same invoice and, where eligible, count it toward that commitment, which may remove a separate procurement step. The practical effect depends on terms the announcement does not give, including price and eligibility, so buyers should confirm them with their account teams. For individual users and small teams nothing changes.
