## What happened

On 23 November 2026 Amazon announced that it signed an agreement to acquire the Decart Optimization Stack, the AI optimization technology of the startup Decart, and to bring on Decart's three co-founders and its team. We read the announcement on Amazon's site. Peter DeSantis of Amazon is quoted in it. The announcement says the deal is subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory approval, and it does not state a price.

The price comes from Israeli outlet Calcalist, which reported the same day that the deal is worth about $7.5 billion in cash and stock. We read the Calcalist article; Amazon has not confirmed the figure.

## Key details

- **Confirmed by Amazon:** a signed agreement to acquire Decart's optimization technology (the Decart Optimization Stack) and to bring over the three co-founders and the team. Closing is not complete and needs regulatory approval, among other conditions.
- **Reported by Calcalist, not by Amazon:** a value of about $7.5 billion in cash and stock, paid in installments, with part of the payout tied to performance, and the founders staying for five years.
- **Decart's background (Calcalist):** it raised $300 million at a valuation of about $4 billion in May, more than $450 million in total, and has about 100 employees.
- **Other bidders (Calcalist):** the outlet reports Anthropic negotiated a deal worth about $6 billion in September and pulled out, and that Nvidia declined to reopen talks. We have not seen either company comment, and these are Calcalist's claims alone.
- **Not confirmed here:** the final price and structure, which Decart products continue, how Amazon will use the technology, and when the deal closes.

## Why it matters

If the reported price is accurate, this is a very large payment for software and people rather than for a model: Amazon says it is acquiring optimization technology and a team. What the Decart Optimization Stack does and how Amazon will use it are not described in the announcement we read.

For now the official facts are limited to an agreement to acquire the technology and the team. The headline number, the competing interest from Anthropic and Nvidia, and the payment terms rest on one outlet's reporting. Watch for Amazon or Decart confirming terms, and for the regulatory review.
