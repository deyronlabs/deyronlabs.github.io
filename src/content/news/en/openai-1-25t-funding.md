## What happened

On 6 November 2026 Reuters wrote that "OpenAI has closed on new funding of $11 billion, the initial part of a round that values the company at $1.25 trillion, Bloomberg News reported on Friday, citing people familiar with the matter." This is a relayed media report, not an announcement by OpenAI. We read the Reuters text, as syndicated by GV Wire, on 9 November 2026. We did not read the Bloomberg article, and the news list on openai.com, whose latest item was dated 6 November, had no announcement about funding or valuation when we checked. Everything below is therefore reported and unconfirmed, attributed to Bloomberg via Reuters.

## Key details

- **Amount:** $11 billion in new funding, described as closed.
- **Round:** the $11 billion is "the initial part" of a larger round, which the report says values OpenAI at $1.25 trillion.
- **Sourcing:** Bloomberg cited people familiar with the matter. Reuters's account does not name the investors, give the total size of the round or say whether the valuation is before or after the new money.
- **Not stated:** the Reuters text does not include a statement from OpenAI, and we did not find other figures about the round in a source we could read. We have not repeated numbers from headlines we could not open.

## Why it matters

If accurate, the report would mean OpenAI's funding is being raised in stages at a very high valuation, which affects how much it can commit to compute, models and products. For people who use ChatGPT, the API or Codex, nothing in the report points to a change in plans, prices or availability. Until OpenAI or the investors confirm the terms, the amount and the valuation should be read as Bloomberg's reporting, relayed by Reuters, and could change.
