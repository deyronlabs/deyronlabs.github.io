## What happened

On 18 November 2026 NVIDIA published a blog post titled "NVIDIA RTX Spark Compact Desktops Out Now". It says RTX Spark compact desktops are available today from ASUS, Dell and MSI. This follows the RTX Spark laptops announced with Microsoft in October. The details below are from NVIDIA's post and are NVIDIA's own claims.

## Key details

- **Availability:** available now from ASUS, Dell and MSI. NVIDIA says Acer, GIGABYTE and HP follow later, along with a "Microsoft Surface RTX Spark Dev Box".
- **Named models:** the post names the ASUS ProArt GR1X mini PC and the MSI EdgeMesa N AI+ Series.
- **Specifications (per NVIDIA):** a 20-core NVIDIA Grace CPU and an NVIDIA Blackwell RTX GPU with 6,144 CUDA cores, up to 128GB of unified memory and up to 1 petaflop of AI performance, plus 10-gigabit Ethernet.
- **Stated use:** running models of up to 120 billion parameters locally.
- **Not stated:** prices, which configurations ship first, and any independent benchmark. The 1 petaflop figure is NVIDIA's and the post does not say at which numeric precision it is measured.

## Why it matters

Compact desktops with up to 128GB of unified memory put large local models, in NVIDIA's words up to 120 billion parameters, within reach of a desk-sized machine, but without published prices or tests the cost and real-world speed remain unknown. Anyone considering one should wait for the partners' price lists and third-party measurements.
