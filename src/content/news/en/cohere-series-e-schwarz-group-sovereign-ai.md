## What happened

On 29 October 2026 Cohere published a post by co-founders Nicholas Frosst and Ivan Zhang and CEO Aidan Gomez announcing "the initial close of its Series E funding round targeting up to CAD $7 Billion, led by Schwarz Group." Cohere lists Bell Ventures, Bullhound Capital, Inovia Capital, NVIDIA, Prosperity7 Ventures and the Government of Canada as other participants, and calls it the largest round ever raised by a private Canadian company. The post does not state the amount closed so far or a valuation.

The post is framed as an argument for "sovereign AI": that countries need their own companies able to build frontier models. These are Cohere's claims, not independent findings.

## Key details

- **Round:** initial close of a Series E "targeting up to CAD $7 Billion," led by Schwarz Group. Some newsletter summaries convert this to about US$5 billion; Cohere's post uses Canadian dollars.
- **Aleph Alpha:** Cohere lists "completing the Aleph Alpha combination" among its plans for the next twelve months. The post does not give terms.
- **AISLE:** Cohere says it plans to acquire AISLE under a letter of intent. Citing AISLE, it says the system found more than 500 CVEs in its first year and identified 18 of the 24 vulnerabilities fixed in curl's October release. Those figures are AISLE's and Cohere's.
- **New products and training:** Cohere says it plans to launch Cohere Cyber for air-gapped environments and to begin frontier-scale training with allied partners.
- **Open-model market claim:** Cohere cites a research paper for the statement that more than 70 percent of open-model tokens served through OpenRouter came from Chinese models earlier this year, up from 3 percent fourteen months earlier.
- **Government spending:** Cohere says Canada has committed more than C$5 billion to AI since 2017 and Germany EUR 5 billion to its national AI strategy since 2018, citing the OECD for the German figure.
- **Government of Canada:** the post lists the Government of Canada as a participant. Press reports, which we have not verified against a government source, put the commitment at up to C$1.6 billion as an equity stake.

## Why it matters

For developers and companies choosing a model supplier, this is a signal that Cohere intends to compete with the largest US and Chinese labs on its own training runs rather than only on enterprise deployment. If the Aleph Alpha and AISLE deals complete, the company would add a European model lab and a security-research team.

Caveats: this is an initial close of a round with a stated target, not a completed raise; the post gives no valuation, no closed amount and no deal terms; and it is a company's own case for state-backed funding, made by a party with a stake in the outcome. We found no independent confirmation of the Aleph Alpha and AISLE plans beyond Cohere's post.
