## What happened

On 6 November 2026 at 7:01 AM Eastern time, President Donald Trump posted on his Truth Social account about “Super Intelligence (SI)”. He wrote: “As President, I am 100% in favor of Super Intelligence (SI)”. According to the archived text, he went on to say that he will work with US states that want to get rich from it, and that “only foolish leadership will say “NO” to the riches they bring!”
