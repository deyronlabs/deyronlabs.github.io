## What happened

NVIDIA published several posts on 9 November 2026 (the page bylines read 8 November) describing an expanded collaboration with South Korea. The main one covers an agreement with Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) spanning sovereign AI, from AI semiconductors and data-center infrastructure to talent, startups, models, agents and security.

NVIDIA also announced a separate collaboration with SK Telecom and chip startup Rebellions, supported by MSIT, on sovereign AI infrastructure using NVIDIA's NVLink Fusion.

## Key details

- **Chips:** the ministry collaboration lays groundwork for Korean NPU and XPU companies to explore joining the NVLink Fusion ecosystem.
- **Models:** through Korea's AI for All initiative, NVIDIA will work with three selected consortia and four Korean foundation-model builders, using NVIDIA Dynamo to optimize and scale inference.
- **Agents and security:** according to NVIDIA, Hyundai Motor Group is exploring NemoClaw blueprints and the OpenShell runtime, and security firm AIM Intelligence is integrating its Starfort agent control platform with OpenShell.
- **LG AI Research:** is opening API access to K-EXAONE-2.5, which NVIDIA says was developed using Nemotron datasets including Nemotron-Personas-Korea, described as an open dataset of 7 million fully synthetic Korean personas.
- **Not stated:** the posts give no GPU counts, investment amounts or timelines. All details are NVIDIA's own descriptions; we did not read statements from the Korean government or the other companies.

## Why it matters

Sovereign AI, meaning national control over AI chips, data centers and models, is a growing driver of demand for NVIDIA hardware and software. The announcement ties Korean chip designers to NVIDIA's interconnect and Korean model builders to its inference stack. Without numbers, it is a statement of direction rather than a measurable commitment.
