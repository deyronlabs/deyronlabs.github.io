## What happened

On 8 November 2026 at 7:05 PM, according to the independent archive trumpstruth.org, President Donald Trump posted on Truth Social that he is “urging all Senators to vote for The American Affordability and Jobs Act (S. 5653)”. He described the bill as a big win for US energy independence and as permitting reform that speeds up pipelines, mines and power plants.

Relevant to AI, the post says the bill also codifies his “Ratepayer Protection Pledge”, which he describes as ensuring that data centers build and pay for their own electricity. He added: “Remember, China will take any Data Center that we don't want, and pay lots of money for it.” He asked readers to call their Senator and vote yes.

Truth Social requires JavaScript, which our tools cannot run, so we read the post through the archive. The original link is the one the archive gives; we did not open it.

## Key details

- **Who and when:** Donald J. Trump (@realDonaldTrump), 8 November 2026, 7:05 PM as shown by the archive.
- **What it says about AI infrastructure:** the bill, as described by Trump, would codify a pledge that data centers cover the cost of their own electricity. The statement about China taking data centers the US does not want is his claim; we have not verified it.
- **What we could not verify:** we could not read the text of S. 5653 (Congress.gov blocked our automated request), so we cannot confirm its title, sponsor, status, or whether and how it covers data-center power costs. Everything about the bill here comes from the post.
- **Related earlier post:** on 6 November Trump posted support for “Super Intelligence”; see our separate article.

## Why it matters

Electricity for data centers is one of the practical limits on AI growth, and who pays for new power capacity is a live policy question. If a federal law did require data centers to build and pay for their own power, it would affect where and how AI companies site large facilities and how those costs are shared with other electricity customers. At this point, though, this is a presidential post about a bill, not a law or a rule, and the bill's actual wording is unconfirmed.

We will update this article if the bill text or a Senate vote becomes available.
