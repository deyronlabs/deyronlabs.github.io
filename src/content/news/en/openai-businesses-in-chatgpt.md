## What happened

On 10 November 2026 OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT, a beta feature that lets companies manage how they appear in ChatGPT and, for some of them, talk to customers through their own agent. We read OpenAI's announcement page on 11 November 2026. Everything below is OpenAI's description of its own product unless stated otherwise.

## Key details

- **Business Hub:** a business can claim its listing, and after verification update its details and add product feeds with prices and availability. It also sees aggregated insights, which OpenAI describes as the number of mentions, clicks to the business's website and "discovery topics".
- **Business Agents:** a conversational agent for the business, kept separate from, and labeled differently than, the regular ChatGPT conversation. It is available now to eligible ecommerce and online-service businesses in the United States.
- **Who can claim:** OpenAI says claiming is open to any eligible business in the countries where ChatGPT is available. Enterprise customers and advertisers are asked to work with their OpenAI representative. Shopify merchants can connect their store from the Business Hub or from the Shopify admin.
- **Ads link:** businesses can sponsor a Business Agent and use it as a destination for ChatGPT Ads, in labeled placements.
- **Privacy:** OpenAI says conversations between users and ChatGPT or a Business Agent are not shared with businesses, and that insights are subject to minimum thresholds.
- **Scale figure:** OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week. This is OpenAI's own number and we have not verified it.
- **Not stated:** pricing for the Business Hub or Business Agents, dates for other countries or business categories (OpenAI says only "over time"), and how many businesses take part.

## Why it matters

The announcement moves ChatGPT further from answering questions toward being a place where businesses are listed, found and sold through, with ads tied to the same agents. For companies in the eligible US categories it offers a way to control the product data ChatGPT shows and to see how often they are mentioned. Because no pricing or rollout dates are given and the feature is in beta, the practical effect depends on terms OpenAI has not published. Users who do not shop or look up businesses in ChatGPT see no change.
