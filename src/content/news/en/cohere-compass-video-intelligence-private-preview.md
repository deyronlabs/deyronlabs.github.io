## What happened

On 16 November 2026 Cohere published a blog post introducing Video Intelligence in Compass, now available in private preview. The feature lets AI applications and agents search a video library and get back timestamped clips, transcripts and visual descriptions alongside results from documents. We read the post on 16 November 2026. The page carries a "noindex" robots tag, so it may not appear in search engines.

## Key details

- **What it does:** search over video returning timestamped clips, transcripts and visual descriptions; the post also mentions a connection through Compass's MCP server.
- **Deployment:** Cohere says customers can use it in a managed cloud or in their own environment.
- **Evaluation, reported by Cohere:** nDCG@10 over 11,133 queries across 11 datasets, compared with seven video embedding models. Cohere says Compass is 4.4 points ahead of the strongest alternative on average.
- **Breakdown, reported by Cohere:** +2.4 versus Amazon Nova Multimodal Embeddings on public benchmarks, +5.1 versus Gemini Embedding 2 on sports, and +2.0 versus Qwen3-VL-Embedding-8B on enterprise data.
- **Availability:** private preview, with design partners being recruited.
- **Not stated:** pricing, a general availability date, a license, and any independent evaluation.

## Why it matters

Video libraries are hard to search with text-only tools, so a retrieval product that returns the exact clip with a timestamp targets a real enterprise problem, such as finding a moment across hours of recorded footage.

The headline comparison is Cohere's own evaluation, and the product is not generally available, so businesses cannot yet test it independently. Whether the lead holds on a company's own footage is the open question.
