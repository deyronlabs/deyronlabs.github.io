## What happened

On 27 October 2026 Google Cloud published a post by Google DeepMind Robotics and Google Cloud staff saying that Gemini Robotics ER 2 is now available in public preview. The post calls it a high-level reasoning model for robots across different physical forms, combining video understanding over time, long-horizon planning and tool orchestration.

## Key details

All details come from the Google Cloud post, read on 29 October 2026. The performance figures are Google's and we have not tested the model.

- **Access:** public preview in Model Garden and Agent Studio, with the model ID `gemini-robotics-er-2-preview`. The post links to documentation and a pricing page but states no prices.
- **Reported figures:** 91.3% accuracy when pinpointing the exact video frame of a critical event, with a mean absolute frame distance of 0.96 seconds, at "4x the execution speed". Google also says the model reads more than ten categories of instruments and can handle multi-minute tasks involving hundreds of decisions.
- **Customers quoted:** Boston Dynamics, DeepReach, Arm and ASTAR IMRE.
- **Wording to note:** the post says the model is available "on the Gemini platform"; the products it names are Model Garden and Agent Studio.

## Why it matters

The model is an orchestration layer for robots rather than a motor-control system: it interprets video, plans and calls tools. A public preview lets developers test those claims on their own footage and tasks, which is the evidence that matters, since the published numbers come from Google's own evaluations.
