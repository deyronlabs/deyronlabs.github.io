## What happened

On 12 November 2026 Anthropic published "Introducing Claude Meetings, now in beta" and said the feature is "starting to roll out" to Team and Enterprise plans. Two days earlier, Anthropic had described Claude Meetings as "coming soon" (see our article on Claude Sheets and Whiteboards); the 12 November post is the first one we found with beta availability and terms.

Claude joins a video call as a visible participant, follows the conversation and can start on work while people are still talking. Support today is Google Meet only; Anthropic says other video platforms are "coming soon."

## Key details

All details are from Anthropic's post, read on 13 November 2026. We have not tested the feature.

- **What it does:** takes notes and action items, answers questions aloud or in chat (in writing by default), builds documents and whiteboards live, and can use connected tools with the user's permission.
- **Who gets it:** Team plans, where inviting Claude is on by default and an admin can turn it off; Enterprise plans, where an admin turns it on for all or part of the organization. Anthropic says some organizations are excluded, including those with zero data retention, a HIPAA-ready configuration or customer-managed encryption keys.
- **Price in beta:** notes and transcripts are free on Team and Enterprise during the beta. Answers are free unless Claude uses a connected tool, which counts toward plan usage. Documents, whiteboards and other artifacts are free through 10 February 2027.
- **Privacy defaults:** by default Claude stays out of calls with people outside the organization and leaves if an outsider joins, unless an admin allows it. Anthropic says it saves a transcript, summary and screen descriptions, not audio. Answers posted in the call chat are visible to everyone.
- **Integrations:** Anthropic names Gong, Attention, Granola, Fireflies.ai, Otter and Plaud as partners for future transcript export.

## Why it matters

For teams on Team or Enterprise plans, this puts an AI participant into meetings with its own data rules, so the admin settings and the outside-guest default matter before anyone switches it on. The free period for artifacts ends on 10 February 2027, and tool use already counts against plan usage, so the cost after the beta is not yet stated.

What is still unknown from the post: availability outside Google Meet, regional availability and pricing after the beta. The privacy claims are Anthropic's description of its own product.
