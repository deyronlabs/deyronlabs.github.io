## What happened

On 6 October 2026, Sierra, in a post by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, announced the Personal Agent Protocol, which it describes as an open standard being developed with Meta and industry partners. It defines how personal AI agents interact with businesses through a company's websites, APIs or its own conversational agent. The post says: "It's open for anyone to implement."

## Key details

- **Partners named in the post:** Rocket, Shopify, Stripe, Genesys and Instinct. Quotes from executives at Genesys, Rocket, Shopify and Stripe appear in the post.
- **Scope:** authentication, consumer control, and company visibility into agent activity. The post's principle is that "consumers decide what access to give their personal agents, and companies set parameters for what those agents can do."
- **How it works as described:** sessions start on the company's website, agents can begin as guests, and account access needs sign-in or stored credentials. The customer chooses read-only or write access. Sessions use OAuth, and agents can reach companies through websites, APIs (MCP and OpenAPI are named) or the company's own agent.
- **Published vs. promised:** only the announcement is published. Sierra says it plans to publish the v0.1 specification "later this month", with design workshops and a reference implementation to follow.
- **Proposed, not committed:** finer-grained permissions, push notifications such as shipping updates, and payment extensions that avoid sharing card details.
- **Not in the post:** a license for the specification or reference implementation, and any adoption numbers.

## Why it matters

Personal agents that book, buy and manage accounts need a shared way to prove who they act for and what they may do. A common protocol would let businesses set rules once instead of per assistant.

For now there is nothing to build against: no specification, license or reference code is public. Whether the standard is truly open will depend on those details, so wait for the v0.1 text before judging it.
