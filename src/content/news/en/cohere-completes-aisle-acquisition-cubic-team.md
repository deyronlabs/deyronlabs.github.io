## What happened

On 13 November 2026 Cohere published a post saying it has completed its acquisition of AISLE, a company building autonomous vulnerability discovery, and is welcoming the team of cubic, an AI code-review company. A letter of intent for AISLE was announced on 15 October 2026.

## Key details

From Cohere's post, read on 14 November 2026.

- **Structure:** AISLE and cubic form a secure-coding and cyber line. Cohere says Ondrej Vlcek becomes SVP of the new Cohere Cyber, Jaya Baloo becomes Chief Information Security Officer and Stanislav Fort becomes VP of AI Security.
- **cubic:** Cohere names Paul Sanglé-Ferrière as cubic's CEO.
- **Prague:** Cohere plans a "substantial long-term investment" in Prague. No figure is given.
- **Pace of deals:** Cohere calls this its fourth acquisition this year, naming Aleph Alpha and Reliant AI among the others.
- **Not disclosed:** the price of either deal or the size of the teams.

## Why it matters

Cohere is positioning itself as an enterprise and sovereign-AI vendor, and security tooling that finds and reviews code vulnerabilities fits that pitch. The acquisitions follow a pattern of AI labs buying application-layer teams rather than building them.

It is a corporate announcement without product details or prices, so the open questions are what Cohere Cyber will actually ship and when.
