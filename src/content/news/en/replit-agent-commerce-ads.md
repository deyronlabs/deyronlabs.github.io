## What happened

On 12 November 2026 Replit published a blog post saying Replit Agent can now buy things for a business and help it reach customers. It adds two features: Agentic Commerce, for purchases, and Agentic Ads, for Google Ads campaigns started from the Replit chat. Everything below is Replit's description.

## Key details

From Replit's post, read in a browser on 14 November 2026. We have not tested the features.

- **Agentic Commerce:** the agent buys business supplies using Link (Stripe) and Visa, and stops at checkout until the user approves the total. Replit says it is available to Core and Pro users, starting in the US, for one-time purchases only.
- **Agentic Ads:** the agent creates Google Ads campaigns across Search, YouTube, Display and Discover from the chat. Replit says it is available to Pro users worldwide from the day of the announcement.
- **How it runs:** Replit says a browser subagent built with Browserbase works in an isolated session and that approval checks are enforced in code.
- **Figures from Replit:** it says Link has more than 300 million users and reports a "ninefold increase" in entrepreneurs connected to Stripe since May. Both numbers are Replit's and we did not check them. Customer quotes in the post are marketing and are not repeated here.
- **Not stated:** prices or credit costs for the features, spending limits, success rates, or any independent evaluation.

## Why it matters

The post is part of the same wave of agentic commerce as OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet, announced a day later: coding and chat agents are being given ways to pay and to advertise. We have not seen evidence that the two are connected. For founders on Replit the draw is doing purchasing and ad setup in the same place as the app.

The open questions are cost and control: what the features consume in credits, how spending is capped, and whether ad campaigns created by an agent perform. It is a niche product for Replit's paying users, so it is mainly a signal of where agent tooling is going.
