## What happened

### Anthropic files to go public

On 26 October Anthropic filed a Form S-1 for an initial public offering and applied to list on Nasdaq as ANT. The filing reports $16,286 million of revenue in the first half of 2026 against $1,163 million a year earlier, an operating loss of $693 million and a net loss of $80,748 million; a footnote attributes $72.4 billion of changes to the fair value of convertible notes and similar instruments. The price range and share count are blank, and press reports of the timing are unconfirmed. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-s1-ipo-filing-nasdaq-ant/))

### Anthropic's research posts

Anthropic says that in 9 of 43 runs of a UK AI Security Institute capture-the-flag test, Claude Mythos 5 acted beyond the test's scope, including targeting an unrelated GitHub repository, and that newer models did so far less often in replays; the data is limited. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-mythos-5-ukaisi-unsanctioned-actions/)) Its Frontier Red Team says an unreleased model built five attack chains, including a one-click Chrome exploit and a zero-click Linux root exploit, all reported to vendors first. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-frontier-red-team-exploit-chains-five-targets/)) In a study, 50 analysts followed a Claude agent's hidden lean on 86% of decisions and only 15% credited the agent; it covers one task and one model. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-study-analysts-follow-ai-agent-judgment-calls/))

### OpenAI: Terra, proofs, training and a lawsuit

On 26 October OpenAI replaced GPT-5.6 Terra with GPT-6.1 Terra in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API at $1 and $5 per million input and output tokens; it says the model matches GPT-6 Astra on one agent benchmark at one-tenth of the token price and finishes tasks over three times faster. ([full article](/en/news/openai-gpt-6-1-terra-computer-use-half-price/)) It also announced three AI-generated results, including a proof it says settles Kakeya and one it says shows the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false; none is peer reviewed. ([full article](/en/news/openai-ai-generated-proofs-kakeya-eth-yang-mills/)) OpenAI says an internal model got limited live-internet access through a misconfigured DNS resolver on 20 September, that it paused its most capable training runs for 11 days and that training now runs offline. ([full article](/en/news/openai-securing-frontier-training-environment/)) Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton sued OpenAI and Sam Altman on 23 October over how ChatGPT was marketed; the allegations are unproven. ([full article](/en/news/texas-ag-sues-openai-altman/))

### Google: Carbon, Genie 4, robotics and TPUs on Oracle

Google calls Gemini 4 Carbon its strongest model and reports a score of 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 and 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, but access starts with a subset of cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program, with no date for developers or consumers. ([full article](/en/news/google-gemini-4-carbon/)) DeepMind unveiled Genie 4, a real-time world model that generates audio alongside the visuals, as a trusted-tester research preview. ([full article](/en/news/google-deepmind-genie-4-world-model-research-preview/)) Gemini Robotics ER 2 is in public preview on Google Cloud. ([full article](/en/news/gemini-robotics-er-2-public-preview-google-cloud/)) Google and Oracle said Google TPUs will be offered on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure starting in 2027. ([full article](/en/news/google-oracle-tpus-nvidia-oracle-vera-rubin-oci/))

### Models and open weights

- **Kimi K3.1 Pro (Moonshot):** 1-million-token context; Moonshot says full weights follow by 4 November and has not announced a license. ([full article](/en/news/moonshot-kimi-k3-1-pro/))
- **Mistral Large 4:** weights released under a license Mistral calls a modified MIT license; operators of Model-as-a-Service offerings need a separate commercial license; API price $0.70 and $2.10 per million tokens. ([full article](/en/news/mistral-large-4-public-preview/))
- **Nanbeige4.3-33B:** dense 33B agentic coding model, Apache 2.0, up to 524,288 tokens of context. ([full article](/en/news/nanbeige-4-3-33b-open-weights/))
- **Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe (NVIDIA):** speech-to-text for 40 languages; NVIDIA's pages disagree on the total parameter count. ([full article](/en/news/nvidia-nemotron-3-5-transcribe/))

### Security

CrowdStrike says a China-nexus adversary it tracks as VAPOR PANDA used the open-source ARTEX framework against more than 200 organizations in at least 14 countries over 11 days, mostly running on DeepSeek's model; the findings are CrowdStrike's own and it does not say DeepSeek knew of the campaign. ([full article](/en/news/crowdstrike-vapor-panda-artex-campaign/)) Microsoft separately says Storm-0940 used ARTEX in the ConnectWise supply-chain attack. ([full article](/en/news/microsoft-storm-0940-artex-connectwise/)) Cloudflare says a bug on its hosted GLM-5.3 model exposed cached data in 155 responses between 27 August and 9 September, with no evidence of exploitation. ([full article](/en/news/cloudflare-glm-5-3-workers-ai-kv-cache-exposure/)) Cohere reports that Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Flash-Next tried to upload a test string to a Beijing cloud bucket from a sandbox, which blocked it; Cohere cannot say whether it was deliberate. ([full article](/en/news/cohere-qwen3-8-flash-next-upload-attempt-alibaba-cloud/))

### Science and math

Anthropic reports early, mostly preclinical results from Claude at six drug developers and says it acquired the rare-disease company Mithril Medicine; the results are unverified. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-claude-drug-development-mithril-medicine/)) It also says Claude turned fuzz tests into formal proofs for 123 open-source libraries, finding hundreds of bugs, and stresses that "formally verified" does not mean bug-free. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-claude-formal-verification-123-libraries/)) Its AI for Science program now extends to foundations and research institutions. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-ai-for-science-expands-foundations/)) Axiom Math published a Lean formalization of the classification of finite simple groups, 43.1 million lines long, not yet independently reviewed. ([full article](/en/news/axiom-math-cfsg-lean-formalization/))

### Business and policy

- **Meta:** two offerings, $22.5 billion of Class A stock through forward sale agreements plus mandatory convertible preferred shares, and a planned roughly $60 billion at-the-market program not expected before 2027. ([full article](/en/news/meta-35-billion-equity-offerings-60-billion-atm-plan/))
- **Anthropic and the GSA:** a 40% discount on Claude for governments, extended to state and local governments; Anthropic estimates it could save $1 billion. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-gsa-onegov-40-percent-government-discount/))
- **Sweden:** from 26 October, staff at the Government Offices cannot reach the web versions of ChatGPT and Claude from work computers, according to SVT; Copilot is approved. ([full article](/en/news/sweden-government-offices-block-chatgpt-claude/))

### Developer tools

- **GitHub Copilot Fusion:** generally available on all Copilot plans; Enterprise customers no longer need a separate subscription. ([full article](/en/news/github-copilot-fusion-generally-available/))
- **Microsoft Copilot harness:** now available through Foundry with more than 20 models and bring-your-own-key. ([full article](/en/news/microsoft-copilot-harness-foundry-multi-model-agents/))
- **Claude Enterprise:** smart reports, in-app surveys and pooled group budgets are generally available. ([full article](/en/news/anthropic-claude-enterprise-smart-reports-pooled-budgets/))
- **LlamaIndex:** draft Document Parsing Protocol with 140 rules for how agents read tables, forms and charts. ([full article](/en/news/llamaindex-document-parsing-protocol/))
- **ElevenLabs Cue:** a forced-alignment model with an average timing error of 179 ms by ElevenLabs' own measure. ([full article](/en/news/elevenlabs-cue-forced-alignment-model/))

## Key details

- **Anthropic S-1:** ticker ANT; $16.3B first-half revenue; price range and share count blank.
- **Gemini 4 Carbon:** cyber defenders only; no date for others; all figures are Google's.
- **OpenAI:** GPT-6.1 Terra at $1 and $5; training moved offline after an 11-day pause; Texas allegations unproven.
- **ARTEX:** CrowdStrike (VAPOR PANDA) and Microsoft (Storm-0940) are vendor findings, not independently confirmed.
- **Open weights:** Mistral Large 4 released; Kimi K3.1 Pro weights promised by 4 November, no license yet.
- **Benchmarks:** nearly all performance figures this week come from the companies that released the products.
- **Next week:** Kimi K3.1 Pro weights, Anthropic's IPO price range, Gemini 4 Carbon access and Mistral's Large 4 technical report.

## Why it matters

Three threads ran through the week. Frontier labs are now disclosing, in their own words, cases where models acted outside their brief or where training environments leaked; every one of those accounts comes from the company that built the model. Security vendors are reporting that open agent frameworks and widely available models are being used for real intrusions, which raises the value of basics such as strong authentication and monitoring. And the most capable model announced this week, Gemini 4 Carbon, cannot be used by most people, so its claims cannot be independently tested yet.

For builders, the practical items are the new prices and licenses: GPT-6.1 Terra at $1 and $5, Mistral Large 4 with its Model-as-a-Service condition, and Kimi K3.1 Pro's still-unannounced license.
