## What happened

Cognition's official Devin documentation now has a product guide for Devin Apps. According to that page, Devin Apps are web apps that Devin builds and serves from its own machine at a stable URL. The documentation lists internal tools, dashboards, forms, trackers and demos as typical uses.

The page carries no date, and Cognition's blog has no dedicated post about the feature (its latest post we found was about SWE-2 Flash, on 4 November), so we cannot say when Devin Apps launched. We read the documentation on 16 November 2026.

## Key details

Everything below is from Cognition's documentation; we have not used the feature.

- **Privacy:** Apps are private. Anyone who opens one must sign in to Devin, and the documentation says they are never public. Access can be limited to people with access, an organization, or an enterprise.
- **How they run:** the app runs on one Devin machine, which goes to sleep five minutes after the last request. The documentation says billing follows the time the machine is awake.
- **Updating:** an app is changed through "Edit in session", and automations can run on a schedule or on a push.
- **Limits:** the documentation says Apps cannot be public, have no service worker, run on a single machine and are not meant for high traffic.
- **Availability:** "Devin Apps are rolling out gradually", and if the sidebar does not show Apps, they are not yet enabled for that user.
- **Not stated:** a price specific to Apps, a launch date, or any usage numbers.

## Why it matters

Devin Apps move the output of a coding agent from code in a repository to something running behind a login: a small internal tool or dashboard that the agent also hosts. For teams, that shortens the path from a request to a usable internal page, at the cost of tying the app to Devin's login and to one machine.

Because the limits are stated plainly (private only, single machine, low traffic), this reads as a feature for internal tools rather than for public products. Pricing and a firm release date are the missing details to watch.
