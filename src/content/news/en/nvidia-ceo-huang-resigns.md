## What happened

In a press release dated 12 November 2026, Nvidia's board announced that Jensen Huang "has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately." Colette Kress, executive vice president and chief financial officer since September 2013, was appointed interim CEO and keeps the CFO role until a permanent successor is named. The board said it is searching for a permanent CEO and will consider internal and external candidates.

The same release names Harvey C. Jones, a former CEO and executive chairman of Synopsys, as the board's first-ever chair. It includes statements from Jones and Kress about continuity of operations and the product roadmap.

## Key details

- **What the source says:** the release states the facts above and does not give a reason for the resignation. We do not repeat unconfirmed press reports about the cause.
- **Earnings:** Nvidia says it will report third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Tuesday 17 November 2026 at about 1:20 p.m. Pacific time, with a call at 2 p.m., "as previously scheduled."

The 12 November release does not say whether the resignation is connected to that investigation, and we have not seen Nvidia say so. ## Why it matters

Nvidia supplies most of the accelerators used to train and run large AI models, so a change of chief executive matters to customers, suppliers and investors, who now look for any shift in its product plans or large deals. Nvidia's own message is that operations, customer commitments and the roadmap continue unchanged.

The near-term markers are on Nvidia's calendar: the 17 November results and call, the timetable for naming a permanent CEO, and any further statement from the independent investigation. Until Nvidia or a regulator says more about the reasons, treat explanations in the press as unconfirmed.
