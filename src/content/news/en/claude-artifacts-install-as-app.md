## What happened

Anthropic published a support article dated 24 November 2026, titled "Turn artifacts into Mac and Windows apps". It describes an "Install as app" option, now in beta, that turns a Claude artifact into an app on the user's computer. We read the article on 25 November 2026.

## Key details

Everything below is from Anthropic's support article; we have not tested the feature.

- **What you get:** an installed artifact has its own icon and its own window. Per the article, it updates when the artifact changes and can be shared.
- **How to use it:** update Claude for Mac or Windows, open an artifact, and choose "Install as app" from the Share menu.
- **Permissions:** there are three levels: Default, App permissions, and Full computer access.
- **Availability:** the beta is on all plans. It is on by default for Team, with full computer access off, and off for Enterprise until an owner turns it on. It is not available to HIPAA-ready organizations or hybrid deployments.
- **Usage limits:** requests made from installed apps count toward the plan's usage limits.
- **Not stated:** a general-availability date or which artifact types are supported.

## Why it matters

Artifacts have been small pages that live inside a conversation. Installing one as an app moves it to the desktop, where it can be reopened without starting a chat. The three permission levels, including full computer access, are the part for administrators to check, which may be why Enterprise starts with the feature off.

The practical question for teams is who is allowed to install and share such apps, and how their usage counts against plan limits.
