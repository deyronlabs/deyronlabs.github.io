## What happened

On 10 November 2026 Anthropic published two product-update articles on claude.com. The first, "Introducing Claude Whiteboards, Claude Sheets, and Claude Data", says Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets are launching in beta, and that Claude Dashboards, which was in beta in October, is now generally available and renamed Claude Data. Anthropic says all three are available on all paid plans, on web, desktop and mobile.

The second article describes "interactive connectors": Anthropic says partners can now embed their apps directly in Claude, opening beside the conversation or full screen, with Adobe, Atlassian, Box and Notion given as examples.

## Key details

Everything below is Anthropic's own description, read from claude.com on 11 November 2026. We have not tested the features. Deyron Labs uses Claude in its workflow, so treat this as a report on a vendor's announcement.

- **Claude Sheets (beta):** Anthropic says it offers "simple spreadsheet functionality" for now and that people can export to .xlsx or CSV for full Excel features. Airtable, Asana, monday.com and Smartsheet are named as partner tools.
- **Claude Whiteboards (beta):** for brainstorming inside Claude; Lucid, Miro and Vani by Zoho are named as export or import partners.
- **Claude Data (generally available):** requires a data connector such as a warehouse or CRM. Certified queries and live sharing are in beta; Anthropic also lists tracked changes, caching with per-dashboard refresh cadences and design-system support. Named partners are Amplitude, Mixpanel, Omni, PostHog and Sigma. The two articles name different example data sources (Redshift, BigQuery and ClickHouse in one; Snowflake and Salesforce in the other).
- **Admin defaults:** on Team plans all three are on by default. On Enterprise plans Whiteboards and Sheets are off by default, and Claude Data turns on by default on 19 November 2026. Admins control them under Organization settings, Artifacts.
- **Marketplace and other items:** Anthropic says Claude Marketplace lists "more than 9,000 connectors and plugins", describes Claude auto-connect as a beta on eligible Team and Enterprise plans, and lists Claude Meetings as "coming soon" without a date.
- **Customer figures are Anthropic's, with no independent check:** for example, an Anthropic legal team's marketing review "from three days down to 24 hours", and a financial-planning team's forecast slides from about 10 hours to under 30 minutes.
- **Not stated:** prices and numeric usage limits for any of the three products.

## Why it matters

For people already paying for Claude, this puts light spreadsheet, whiteboard and dashboard work inside the same app, which may replace some small tasks that previously meant switching tools. Anthropic itself says Sheets is basic for now and points to exports for anything advanced, so it is not a substitute for a full spreadsheet application yet.

For organizations, the defaults matter more than the features: Enterprise admins must switch on Whiteboards and Sheets, and Claude Data will switch on by default on 19 November, so administrators who do not want it need to act before then. Because the announcement does not state usage limits or pricing, it is unclear whether heavy use will count against existing plan limits.
