## What happened

On 5 November 2026 Anthropic published "Introducing Personal Finance in Claude". The company says the feature "starts rolling out in beta this week, on the Pro, Max, and Ultra plans in the US." It lets a user connect bank accounts and ask Claude questions about their own money in plain language. The article gives no price for the feature and names no countries beyond the US. We read Anthropic's announcement on 9 November 2026.

## Key details

- **How accounts connect:** the user signs in to their bank through Plaid, a service that connects apps to banks, agrees to Plaid's terms and approves Claude's access there. Anthropic says Claude does not ask for or see the bank password.
- **Read-only:** per Anthropic, "Access is read-only, and Personal Finance can't move, manage, or invest your money." The company also says the feature is not designed to give investment advice.
- **What Claude sees:** for the accounts the user chooses, the bank name, each account's name, type and last four digits, balances and credit limits, and up to two years of transactions.
- **What it can do:** Anthropic's examples include totalling monthly spending by category, comparing it with earlier months, listing payments due before payday, spotting possible duplicate charges, unexpected fees or bills that rose over the year, and calculating interest paid for a given APR. Claude can also build spending charts, a calendar of due bills or a dashboard the user keeps adjusting.
- **Privacy:** Anthropic says conversations in the Personal Finance hub "aren't used to train our models"; chats outside the hub follow the user's normal privacy settings. Users can unlink an account at any time, after which Claude can no longer see its data. The announcement does not state a retention period and points to a Help Center article for details, which we did not read.
- **Stance and limits:** Anthropic says there are no ads or sponsored recommendations, and that the feature does not offer credit cards, loans or savings accounts. It warns that Claude can make mistakes and that users should check which transactions sit behind a number and verify important figures with their bank.

## Why it matters

This moves Claude from answering general questions to working on a user's real financial data, which makes accuracy and data handling the main things to judge. The read-only design limits what can go wrong, since Claude cannot move money, but a wrong figure could still lead to a wrong decision, which is why Anthropic itself advises checking numbers with the bank. For readers outside the US, on the free plan or on Team and Enterprise plans, the announcement lists no availability. Retention terms are not in the announcement, so users who care about them should read the Help Center article before linking an account.
