## What happened

On 17 November 2026 NVIDIA published an announcement that its board of directors "has established the National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee", described as a new standing committee responsible for board-level oversight of the company's national security policies. The announcement is NVIDIA's own; this article relies on it and on nothing else about the committee's work.

## Key details

- **Chair and members:** director Suzanne Nora Johnson chairs the committee; the other named members are Stephen C. Neal and John O. Dabiri.
- **Mandate:** according to NVIDIA, oversight of national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management.
- **Operation:** the committee meets at least quarterly, including in executive session, and will work with independent outside experts in law, national security and trade compliance.
- **Reporting lines:** NVIDIA's internal National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team report to the committee and to Interim CEO Colette Kress.
- **Statement:** Chairman Harvey Jones is quoted saying the company is "committed to working constructively with government and industry partners."
- **Not stated in the announcement:** any link to a specific investigation, any change to what NVIDIA ships to China, or a timeline for naming a permanent CEO. NVIDIA's Q3 results release, issued the same day, guides Q4 revenue on the assumption of no Data Center compute revenue from China.

## Why it matters

NVIDIA's export compliance now has a named board-level owner at a time when its leadership is in transition and U.S. rules on advanced AI chips keep changing. For customers and regulators the open question is how the committee's oversight shows up in practice, since the announcement describes structure and mandate rather than any specific policy change.
