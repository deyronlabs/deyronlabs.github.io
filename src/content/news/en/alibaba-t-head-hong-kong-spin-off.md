## What happened

In an announcement dated 10 November 2026, filed with the SEC as Exhibit 99.1, Alibaba said its wholly owned subsidiary T-Head (Shanghai) Technology Co., Ltd. submitted a confidential Form A1 listing application to the Hong Kong stock exchange on 11 September 2026, for a proposed spin-off and separate listing on the Main Board. Alibaba describes T-Head as a leading provider of full-stack data center chips.

## Key details

All details are from Alibaba's announcement, read on 12 November 2026.

- **Structure:** a Hong Kong public offering plus an institutional placing of T-Head H shares. Alibaba expects T-Head to remain its subsidiary afterward.
- **Not final:** the size and structure of the offering "have not yet been finalized". No revenue, profit or valuation figures are given.
- **Approvals:** the proposal still needs approval from the exchange's Listing Committee and completion of a China Securities Regulatory Commission filing. Alibaba says there is no assurance the spin-off will take place. The exchange granted a waiver on shareholders' assured entitlement.
- **Alibaba's listings:** Alibaba expects its own Hong Kong and New York listings to be unaffected.

## Why it matters

T-Head designs the chips Alibaba uses in its own data centers, so a listing would be a rare window into the financials of a Chinese AI chip supplier. For now there is nothing to analyze: no figures, no timetable and no guarantee. Watch for the public listing documents, which would be the first source of numbers.
