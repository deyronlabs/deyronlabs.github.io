## What happened

On 23 November 2026 (filed at 4:15 pm Eastern) the Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS), part of the U.S. Department of Commerce, filed a final rule with the Federal Register that adds seven entities to the Entity List. The document, "Additions to the Entity List" (number 2026-23999, Docket No. 261123-0006), is scheduled for publication on 25 November 2026. We read the public inspection PDF on 24 November 2026. The rule is not yet published in the daily Federal Register, and the version we read leaves the effective date as a placeholder, so the final date is not confirmed here.

## Key details

- **Entities added (as printed in the rule):** in Malaysia, MSFusion Sdn. Bhd., PT Megaspeed International and Speedmatrix Sdn Bhd.; in Singapore, Apex Enterprise Solutions (Singapore) Private Limited, Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd. (aliases include "Megaspeed" and "7Road International") and Swiftdata Pte. Ltd.; in Thailand, Megaspeed (Thailand) Co., Ltd.
- **Stated reason:** BIS says the entities pose a "significant risk" of being or becoming involved in the diversion of advanced computing commodities to unauthorized end uses or end users.
- **Effect:** a license requirement for all items subject to the Export Administration Regulations, with a license review policy of "presumption of denial" for every entry. A savings clause lets shipments already en route finish within a set number of days; the number is a placeholder in the version we read.
- **What the text does not say:** the rule does not name Nvidia, GPUs or any specific chip, and we found no mention of the foreign direct product rule in the text we read.
- **Context, from press reports:** TechCrunch, citing Semafor, reported on 23 November that Megaspeed was headed for the Entity List, that it is a Singapore-based cloud and AI infrastructure provider, and that Commerce had opened a criminal probe into whether it smuggled Nvidia chips to China. TechCrunch also reported that the company denies wrongdoing. We did not verify these press claims against a court or agency document.
- **Not read:** the full Federal Register notice after publication, any BIS press release, and any statement from Megaspeed or Nvidia.

## Why it matters

For AI developers and cloud users the direct effect is limited: the rule restricts who may supply U.S.-origin items to these seven entities, and it says nothing new about ordinary customers. It matters as a concrete enforcement step in the chip-diversion story that has surrounded Nvidia's customers and leadership (see our earlier coverage of [Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang](/en/news/nvidia-huang-reinstated-ceo/)). The facts to watch are the published effective date, any Commerce or Nvidia statement, and whether further listings follow.
