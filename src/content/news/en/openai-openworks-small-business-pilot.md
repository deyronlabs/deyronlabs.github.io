## What happened

OpenAI introduced OpenWorks, described on its page as a nationwide network of OpenWorks Partners that provide free, hands-on AI support to small businesses. The page says the pilot is "now live" in the communities it lists and that OpenAI is recruiting its first partners. The page carries no date, so we cannot confirm the launch day; The Rundown AI newsletter reported it on 24 November 2026. We read the OpenAI page on 24 November 2026.

## Key details

- **Communities (20, as listed by OpenAI):** Chicago, Kansas City, Fargo, Madison, Cleveland, South Bend, St. Louis, Jackson, Charlotte, Oklahoma City, Charleston, Nashville, Austin, Houston, Atlanta, Los Angeles, Incline Village, Olympia, Seattle and New York.
- **Partners:** technically skilled individuals and small teams; the page says they receive "a monthly stipend of up to $8,000", dedicate days each month to local businesses and host a monthly workshop. Software and AI consultancies are encouraged to apply alongside existing client work.
- **Businesses:** independently owned U.S. small businesses such as restaurants, retailers, service firms and manufacturers get the help for free, with "3 months of ChatGPT Business at no cost". Software and SaaS companies, venture-backed businesses and private-equity-owned businesses are not eligible.
- **Not stated on the page:** a launch date, an application deadline, how many partners will be selected, or how results will be measured.

## Why it matters

The program is a distribution effort: it puts human helpers and a free three-month trial in front of small employers who may not have tried ChatGPT Business. For owners it means free help in the listed communities if they qualify; for partners it is a paid way into AI consulting. It is a pilot with a limited footprint, and OpenAI has not published outcomes, so the useful signal will be whether it expands beyond the 20 communities.
