## What happened

On Friday 20 November 2026, at a White House press conference, President Donald Trump said that American companies will be barred from building data centers in other countries, and that those that do will pay a “tremendous tax”. We read the CNBC report by Jordan Novet and Annie Palmer; we did not find an official transcript or White House document. The claims below are Trump's statements as CNBC reports them, not a policy we could check.

CNBC quotes him: “they can, but we're going to put a tremendous tax on them.” He also said: “I haven't even said that to my people. But I'm telling them now they're here.” CNBC writes that the policy “apparently came as news to his own staff”. It adds that the statement came a day after the meeting with Xi and Putin in China that CNBC refers to.

## Key details

- **Who and when:** President Donald Trump, 20 November 2026, at a White House press conference, as reported by CNBC.
- **What was said:** US companies would be barred from building data centers abroad; those that do would face a “tremendous tax”. The two parts are not reconciled in the quotes CNBC gives: the quote above says companies “can” build abroad but would be taxed.
- **What is not specified:** the CNBC article gives no tax rate, no start date, no legal mechanism, no executive order and no official document. We found no text of the policy.
- **Companies affected:** CNBC notes that Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta and Oracle already operate data centers outside the US. It reports that representatives of Amazon, Google, Microsoft and Oracle did not immediately respond to requests for comment.
- **Public opinion cited by CNBC:** in the CNBC All-America Economic Survey, 64% of registered voters said they oppose an AI data center within 10 miles of them and 13% support one.

## Why it matters

For now this is a spoken statement, not a rule. Companies cannot plan around a tax with no rate, date or legal basis, and the quotes leave open whether the aim is a ban, a tax or both.

If a measure does follow, it would matter to the hyperscalers that already run data centers abroad and to anyone who relies on AI services served from those sites. Until a text exists, the practical effect is uncertainty. We will update this article if an official document or company response appears.
