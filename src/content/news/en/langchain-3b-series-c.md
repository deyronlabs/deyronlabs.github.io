## What happened

On 13 November 2026 LangChain published a post by co-founder Harrison Chase announcing that it has raised $300 million in a Series C at a $3 billion valuation. LangChain makes tools for building and running AI agents, including the LangSmith platform. We read the company post on 14 November 2026.

## Key details

- **Investors:** the round was led by CapitalG, an existing investor. LangChain says GIC and DFJ Growth are new investors with "significant" participation, and that IVP and Sapphire Ventures, both existing investors, took part. It also welcomes Salesforce Ventures, Insight Venture Partners, QuantumLight and MongoDB Ventures, and says Sequoia, Amplify and Benchmark are doubling down.
- **Customers, as stated by LangChain:** more than 7,000 organizations use LangSmith, with Harvey, Rippling, Listen Labs, NVIDIA, Toyota and Salesforce named. These figures come from the company and are not independently verified.
- **Examples from the post:** LangChain says Clay runs hundreds of millions of agent runs a month and that Workday cut the time to launch an agent from five months to under a day. Both are the company's own claims.
- **Products named:** LangSmith Agent Server, Sandboxes, Managed Agents, Fleet, Engine, Fine-Tuning, and LLM and Tool Gateways. LangChain says it will hold a launch event on Friday 20 November 2026.
- **Not stated:** how the money will be spent in figures, revenue, pricing, or any product availability that is new.

## Why it matters

The round shows investors still putting large sums into the tooling layer around AI agents, not only into model labs. For teams already building on LangChain, the post signals a push toward hosted, managed agent infrastructure.

The announcement does not include revenue or usage details beyond the company's own customer count, so the valuation is a measure of investor confidence rather than of disclosed financials. The 20 November event is where product details are expected.
