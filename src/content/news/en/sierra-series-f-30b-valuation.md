## What happened

On 23 November 2026 Sierra announced a Series F round in a post by co-founders Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor. The company says it is raising $1.75 billion at a $30 billion valuation, led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners. We read Sierra's post; the figures below are company statements.

Sierra says earlier lead investors Benchmark, Sequoia, Greenoaks, GV and Tiger Global are also "doubling down".

## Key details

- **Round (Sierra):** $1.75 billion Series F at a $30 billion valuation; the round is described as being raised, not as closed.
- **Revenue (Sierra):** over $400 million in annual recurring revenue in "just over two and a half years".
- **Pricing model (Sierra):** the company says it charges for outcomes rather than usage or tokens.
- **Earlier round:** the same page points to a 4 May 2026 round of $950 million at a valuation of over $15 billion.
- **Not confirmed here:** the revenue figure and customer numbers have not been independently verified, and we did not see a statement from the investors.

## Why it matters

The valuation doubled from the earlier round in under seven months, according to the figures on Sierra's own page, which makes Sierra one of the most highly valued companies focused on customer-service agents. The revenue claim is what supports that price, and it is unaudited.

Sierra's outcome-based pricing, if it holds at scale, differs from the per-token or per-seat models most AI vendors use. Watch for confirmation of the round's close and for independent reporting on revenue and customer retention.
