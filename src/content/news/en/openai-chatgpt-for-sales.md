## What happened

On 17 November 2026 OpenAI published "Introducing ChatGPT for Sales". The announcement describes a Sales plugin for ChatGPT and "more than 30" new and expanded partner plugins that connect ChatGPT to the tools sales teams already use.

## Key details

- **Partner plugins named by OpenAI:** CRM tools Salesforce, HubSpot, Close CRM and monday CRM; sales outreach tools Apollo, Clay, Common Room, Outreach and Salesloft; call and meeting tools Gong, Otter.ai and Granola; contract and quoting tools Docusign, Ironclad, PandaDoc and ServiceNow CPQ; and design tools Gamma, Figma and Canva. The list in the announcement continues beyond these names.
- **"Dot":** OpenAI describes an always-on agent inside ChatGPT, called "dot", as part of the offering.
- **Admin control:** on Business and Enterprise plans, administrators turn plugins on from the Admin Console under Plugins. OpenAI says plugin availability varies by plan, region and settings.
- **Not stated:** a price, the exact plans eligible for the Sales plugin, and performance figures. The only results on the page are a customer quote.

## Why it matters

For sales teams that already work in a CRM, the practical question is whether ChatGPT can read and update that CRM through a plugin instead of copy-and-paste. The announcement shows OpenAI relying on a large partner list rather than building its own sales tools. Until OpenAI states which plans include the plugin and what it costs, a team cannot judge whether it is worth switching on. Administrators should check their own Plugins settings, since availability differs by plan and region.
