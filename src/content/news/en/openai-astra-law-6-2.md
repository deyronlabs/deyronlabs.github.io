## What happened

On 17 November 2026 OpenAI introduced Astra for Law. OpenAI describes it as a configuration of GPT-6.2 Astra with a legal search index and instructions for legal tasks. It is available on request to ChatGPT Enterprise customers through OpenAI's Trusted Access Program and the admin console. In the API it needs separate approval through an account manager. It is not publicly available, and OpenAI states it is not legal advice. All results below are OpenAI's own.

## Key details

- **Harvey LAB-AA launch comparison (OpenAI):** on a comparison run with Artificial Analysis on 120 tasks across 24 areas of law, OpenAI reports a full-score rate of 20.8% for Astra for Law, against 15.0% for GPT-6.2 Astra and 13.1% for Muse Wonder Full.
- **Hallucinations (OpenAI):** OpenAI reports 0.03 substantial hallucinations per task for Astra for Law against 0.59 for Claude Opus 5.5, which it describes as 95% fewer.
- **Vals AI Legal Research Bench (OpenAI):** about 46% rising to 54.81%.
- **LAB USCL Elo (OpenAI):** 1,844 against 1,817 for Opus 5.5 on research done by the model itself, and 1,883 against 1,882 when given precedents.
- **Index:** OpenAI says it added about 539,000 US resources and about 98,000 resources from the UK, Australia and Canada.
- **Not stated:** price, and independent replication of the results.

## Why it matters

For law firms and legal departments already on ChatGPT Enterprise, this is a specialised legal setup of OpenAI's top model that they can ask OpenAI to enable. The comparisons are OpenAI's own, on evaluations it chose, and include a rival model from Anthropic, so they say little about how it performs on a firm's own matters. Legal work still needs human review. Use the numbers as a reason to run a pilot on your own documents, not as evidence that the tool can be trusted unchecked.

> Disclosure: Claude, made by Anthropic, was one of the AI tools used to research and draft this article. Anthropic makes Claude Opus 5.5, which OpenAI compares against. This is not legal advice. All results are OpenAI's own.
