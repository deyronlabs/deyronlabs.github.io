## What happened

On 25 November 2026, at its NIO IN 2026 event in Guangzhou, NIO presented **NIO WorldModel (NWM) 3.0**, the model behind its driver-assistance system, together with **NOMI Intelligence 4.0**, an in-car assistant and agent framework. NIO said NWM 3.0 will reach owners from December, "upon regulatory filing and clearance".

## Key details

- **Efficiency (NIO's figures):** NWM 3.0 uses 4D spatiotemporal compression, which NIO says reduces the compute needed for attention on the vehicle by 36 times. It reports chassis-control latency down 78% and total vehicle-control latency down 54%.
- **Training:** reinforcement learning in two closed loops (the observation space and the latent space) with a reward model. NIO reports 5x training efficiency over its predecessor, 40% fewer driver interventions and a roughly 60% rise in the "sense of reassurance" users report. It does not state the methodology behind these figures.
- **Rollout:** from December for users of the Banyan, Cedar, Cedar S and Coconut+ systems, subject to regulatory filing and clearance.
- **NOMI Intelligence 4.0:** a proprietary foundation model plus an in-vehicle agent framework. NIO says Cedar 2.0.0 will be filed in December and released after approval, with Cedar S and Banyan in the second quarter of 2027, and Coconut and Coconut+ (ONVO) later.
- **Internal AI use:** NIO said it runs more than 7,500 internal AI agents with 2.9 million executions.
- **Not stated:** benchmark details, model parameter counts, independent evaluation or any regulatory approval already obtained.

## Why it matters

This is applied AI in cars, not a general-purpose language model, and it is the kind of claim that is hard to check from outside: the headline numbers are NIO's own and come without a method. The concrete thing to watch is the regulatory step. The December rollout depends on filing and clearance, so the real test is whether owners get the update on time and whether driver-intervention data is ever reported independently.
