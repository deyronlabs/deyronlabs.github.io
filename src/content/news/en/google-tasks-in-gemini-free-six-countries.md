## What happened

In a 17 November 2026 post by Josh Woodward, Google said Tasks in the Gemini app is now free in six countries (the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India) and is rolling out to what the post calls "hundreds of millions of people". New advanced features start in the U.S. The details below are Google's.

## Key details

- **Model and setup:** Tasks runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Users switch the home toggle from Chat to Task. Google says tasks run in hardware-isolated virtual machines on Google Cloud's Agent Compute Engine and connect to more than 60 apps.
- **U.S. features:** phone calls to businesses (not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana or Nebraska), purchases with a spending cap through AP2 (American Express is "coming soon"), SMS and RCS texting (needs a U.S. phone number) and email forwarding to gemini@google.com.
- **Not stated:** usage limits on the free tier, how failed or disputed purchases are handled, and availability dates outside the six countries.

## Why it matters

Agent features that were behind paid plans become available to free users in several large markets, which raises the stakes for how safely an agent can spend money and place calls. Google's spending cap and state restrictions are the visible safeguards; the post does not describe error handling.
