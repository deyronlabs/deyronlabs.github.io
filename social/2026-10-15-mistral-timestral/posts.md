## LinkedIn (pagina DeyronLabs) — linkedin-1200x627.png
Mistral AI introduced Timestral on 15 October, its first family of time-series foundation models for probabilistic forecasting: 5M, 28M, 123M and 209M parameters, a 32,768 time-step context and a modified MIT license.

What Mistral reports: the 209M model ranks first on GIFT-Eval, TIME and fev-bench, and a one-day-ahead forecast takes 10 ms (5M) to 140 ms (209M) on a server CPU with 8 threads.

What is worth noting: Mistral ran the evaluations itself, and the technical report on its main method is still to come. Results on your own data will matter more than leaderboard rank.
