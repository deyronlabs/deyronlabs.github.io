## LinkedIn (pagina companiei)
Xiaomi has released MiMo-V2.6, a family of multimodal models with open weights: Pro (1.02T parameters, 42B active) and Flash (309B, 15B active), both with a 1M-token context and an MIT license tag on Hugging Face.

What is independently measured: Artificial Analysis lists Pro at 46 on its Intelligence Index (Kimi K3 44, GLM-5.3 45, leading closed models 53 according to Xiaomi's comparison).

What is Xiaomi's own claim: the agent and coding benchmarks, the comparison to Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, and the training cost. Xiaomi's announcement and model card also differ on some figures.

Practical note: Pro needs a multi-GPU cluster, so most teams will use the cheap API ($0.43/$0.87 per million tokens) or the smaller models.
