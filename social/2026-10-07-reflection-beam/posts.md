## LinkedIn (pagina companiei)
Reflection has announced Beam, an open-weight mixture-of-experts model (501B total parameters, 23B active).

What is confirmed by Reflection's own announcement: the architecture, the benchmark table, and a promise to release the weights under Apache 2.0 later in October after final red-teaming.

What is not yet verifiable: the weights themselves, the license text, and any independent evaluation. The headline claim is efficiency (comparable to GLM-5.2 at 3–4x less inference compute), not the top score: in Reflection's own table Beam trails Kimi K3, GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on several coding benchmarks.

Our read: the efficiency claim is the part to watch once the weights are public.
