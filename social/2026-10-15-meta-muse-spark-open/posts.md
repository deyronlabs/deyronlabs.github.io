## LinkedIn (pagina DeyronLabs) — linkedin-1200x627.png
Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Spark Open on 15 October: a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 816B total parameters (42B active), a 1M-token context and downloadable weights.

What Meta reports: coding and agent strength (MCP-Atlas 89.2), and a Muse Model License that allows use, fine-tuning and redistribution, with a separate agreement required from companies above $20 million in revenue that offer it as a service.

What is worth noting: "most capable US open-weight model" is Meta's wording. On Meta's own table, Kimi K3 and GLM 5.3 score higher on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (44 and 45 vs 39), and the smallest official quantization needs about 700GB of GPU memory.
