xAI (now SpaceXAI) announced Grok Voice 3.0 on 14 October, a speech-to-speech model for its Voice Agent API.

What xAI reports: $0.08 per minute, unchanged from Grok Voice 2.0; latency "under one second" (no exact figure); an Artificial Analysis Speech-to-Speech Index of 86.9 against 83.2 for GPT-Live-1.

What is worth noting: the table is xAI's own choice of tests, and GPT-Live-1 still leads on τ-Voice (74.5% vs 71.6%). Teams using the grok-voice-latest alias were switched automatically, so it is worth re-testing call quality.
