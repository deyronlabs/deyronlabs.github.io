## LinkedIn (pagina DeyronLabs)
Small active-parameter models are what make local AI agents practical. Liquid AI's new LFM2.5-24B-A2B has 24B parameters but activates about 2B per token, with a 131K-token context.
Liquid's own benchmarks: ahead on 5 of 8 agentic tests (for example 27.69 on AutomationBench vs 8.72 for the next model), behind Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on SWE-Bench Verified (51.00 vs 66.20) and GPQA Diamond (74.42 vs 83.69). Liquid's model card says it is not recommended for knowledge-heavy tasks on its own.
Not stated: independent results, and what the lfm1.0 license allows commercially.
