## LinkedIn (pagina DeyronLabs)
Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published research on whether small AI models could replicate across low-cost IoT devices without a datacenter.
Its results: a 26B model ran across 15 $55 TV boxes at 0.39 tokens per second; a 9B model got inference running on 8 of 9 hardware types; in an 8-device emulated network GLM-5.2 spread to all nodes in 4 of 5 trials.
Caveats the authors state: the access step was trivial (an upper bound), the timeline is a rough trend estimate, and the botnet-scale figures rest on one device class.
Their defense advice is ordinary: anomaly detection, quarantine, better default IoT security.
